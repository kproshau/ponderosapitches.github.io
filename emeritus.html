Emeritus Members

With gratitude to the singers who helped shape The Ponderosa Pitches.

  • Allie Glover

    Altoshe/her

    Allie is a folk singer songwriter. She loves singing in community and leading Kirtan.

  • Elsa Hager

    Sopranoshe/her

    Asst. Music Director, Jan–May 2026

    Elsa’s love for making noise began when she started humming tunes from The Sound of Music at age one and a half. She has since gained (and subsequently lost) some solid piano skills, acquired an undergraduate music theory degree, and sang with the Whitman College Chorale, Cor de Dones (in Palma de Mallorca, Spain), and the Central Oregon Mastersingers. She is thrilled to have been one of the inaugural members of the Pitches, and hopes to find a group half as lovely and talented during her next chapter in North Carolina.

  • Heather Reichert

    Sopranoshe/her

    Heather is a lifelong musician and holds a B.A. in Vocal Performance and Composition from Middlebury College. After a long hiatus from performance, she joined the Central Oregon Mastersingers in 2022 and pretty much sings every chance she gets.

  • Sonia Adams

    Sopranoshe/her

    New to Central Oregon but not new to voice. Sonja has been singing since she could mutter a sound. She nurtured her vocal skills in high school and decided to go to Eastern Oregon University to further her knowledge and earn a Bachelor of Music. Sonja feels as though music is one of the special things in life that can make humans experience pure joy - she can’t wait to share that joy with you.
