Book the Pitches

Interested in having The Ponderosa Pitches perform at your event? Send us an inquiry and we'll be in touch.

The inquiry form asks for:

  • Your event date and venue
  • The type of setting
  • Your expected audience
  • How long you'd like us to sing
  • Any music or theme preferences
