About

Elevated treble music made with heart and intention

Our Story

Founded out of friendships forged in the Central Oregon Mastersingers' choir rows, The Ponderosa Pitches began as an idea from singer Sarah Flagg. She was particularly interested in starting a local treble a cappella group that explored music beyond the traditional choir and church choir offerings that were already deeply rooted in the Central Oregon community. The vision for the Pitches took shape over coffee and conversations in late 2025–early 2026 with fellow singers Riley Sheets and Lauren J., both equally as enthusiastic as Sarah about finding a way to make this passion project a reality.

Bringing together talented singers from Central Oregon Mastersingers and the broader Central Oregon community, the Pitches held their first rehearsal on January 18, 2026, at Nativity Lutheran Church in Bend. Just two months later, they made their public debut in downtown Bend on March 21, 2026.

Today, the Ponderosa Pitches continue to expand their treble a cappella repertoire across a variety of genres while performing throughout Central Oregon and look forward to continuing opportunities to connect with the community!
