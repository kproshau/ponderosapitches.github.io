About the group

The Ponderosa Pitches are a high-level treble a cappella group based in Central Oregon, built on full acceptance, strong community, and a shared commitment to musical excellence. We're looking for skilled, curious singers who love harmony, value kindness, and want to be part of a group that challenges itself while truly supporting one another.

Who we're looking for

  • Advanced-level singers with previous choral, a cappella, or group singing experience. That experience shapes our repertoire and the pace of rehearsal.
  • Comfort reading sheet music, which we use for most, if not all, pieces.
  • Singers who share our values of inclusivity, kindness, and respect. We are an inclusive group with members of many different identities and backgrounds.

What to expect at an audition

  1. Solo selection

    Prepare a 30-second solo: one verse and one chorus. Pop is preferred, and folk or rock are also welcome. Please don't perform opera, art song, or classical arias.

  2. Vocal range check

    We'll guide you through a few simple warmups to find your vocal range.

  3. Note check

    We'll play a set of notes and ask you to sing them back to us.

  4. Blend and harmony

    You'll learn a short verse with one of our members in your preferred vocal range. Then you'll sing that part with members from other voice parts to test blend and harmony, first in a large group and then as the only one on your part.

Good to know

  • We rehearse on Tuesday evenings in Southeast Bend. Please be ready to discuss any possible or recurring conflicts with that time at your audition.
  • New members who receive an offer go through a probationary period, so we can make sure the group is a good fit both musically and culturally.
