    Sopranoshe/her

    Favorite artist

    Favorite tree

    Favorite Pitches song

    Musical fun fact
    Country is her go-to for karaoke!
    Why I love the Pitches
    She loves singing with such amazing and talented humans. It's so challenging and fun to create beautiful music.
    Read Christina's bio

    Christina was born and raised in Bend, Oregon. Thrown onto the stage to sing as young as age 5, she always loved being in front of people. It wasn't until she was listening to "I Will Survive" on a tape recorder that she realized she loved singing solo. Thus began her lifetime of singing and the love of performing, from competitions at the Deschutes County Fair to jazz choir at Mountain View High School. Although she spends her work days as a family nurse practitioner in functional medicine, the joy of singing will always be a part of her life. She is so excited and honored to be a part of this group.

    Jay's musical background is diverse. They grew up singing for anyone who would listen, and what began as a childhood passion evolved into a lifelong love of music. Their experience includes classical choral and solo singing, as well as Americana, folk, rock, pop, funk, and jazz. Over the years, Jay has performed with numerous small ensembles, bands, and choirs. Most notably, they sang with Consonare Chorale in Portland, Oregon, for eight years. When they're not singing with Ponderosa Pitches, Jay can be found reading a good book, dancing, or outdoors hiking and spending time near the water. They also sing in an a cappella folk trio, work as a massage therapist, and enjoy spending time with their beloved friends and family.

  • Mariah Runk

    Mezzoshe/her

    Co-Music Director · Arranger

    Favorite artist
    Eric Whitacre
    Favorite tree
    Paper birch
    Favorite Pitches song
    Hercules Medley
    Fun fact
    She played a viola solo at Carnegie Hall!
    Why I love the Pitches
    Making beautiful music with so many talented artists and incredible people!
    Read Mariah's bio

    Music has been a part of Mariah's life for what feels like forever. She started piano at age 6, singing at age 10, and viola at age 11. During her freshman year in high school, her choral teacher essentially told her to get into voice lessons immediately, leading to her eventual roles throughout the school's many musical groups as section leader, principal alto, student director, and co-founder of the Women's Quartet. She followed this musical path to Whitworth University, participating in the Whitworth Choir and the Whitworth Chamber Singers, and placed at the Northwest NATS competition. After graduating, she sang with the Spokane Choral Artists and Spokane Kantorei before moving to Bend in 2017, where she now sings with the Central Oregon Mastersingers and Ponderosa Pitches.

    Originally from Maine, Sarah has been active in the Central Oregon performing arts community since late 2022. In New England, she performed at a variety of regional theaters, including Rochester Opera House, Prescott Park Arts Festival, Firehouse Center for the Arts, Hackmatack Playhouse, and CastAside Productions. They also have experience in collegiate a cappella (Mount Holyoke College's Victory Eights), group vocal jazz, and pop/rock vocals. Her onstage experience is complemented by ongoing work behind the scenes, serving as everything from deck crew to non-profit board member. Most recently, they have been enjoying freelancing as a music and/or stage director with performers of all ages. For full credits, upcoming projects, training, and biography, visit sflagg.com.
