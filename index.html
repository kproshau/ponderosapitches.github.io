Central Oregon's newest treble a cappella group

The Ponderosa Pitches exist to give advanced treble singers a space to make exceptional music together. We strive to cultivate a high-level, joyful, and sustainable vocal ensemble that centers integrity, modern sound, and a collaborative music-making community.
